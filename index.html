I am a PhD student in the Philosophy department at Johns Hopkins University, working on German Idealism and Early Modern philosophy. I am writing a dissertation on Kant’s account of the imagination, supervised by Lucy Allais.

In 2025–26, I was a DAAD fellow at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin. Prior to coming to Hopkins, I studied Physics and Philosophy at King’s College London.
